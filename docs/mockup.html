Edge-case behavior

  • Multiple sends: sent bubbles preserve separate timestamps and delivery status.
  • Subject changes: keep under a thread only when message IDs connect them.
  • Forwarding: show a distinct attachment/forward bubble; do not merge with original sender history.
  • No-reply: disable compose and require confirmation if a sender address is overridden.
  • Mailing list: show the list address separately and offer Reply vs Reply all.
  • Newsletter: detect no-reply, bulk, and list headers; open page reader instead of chat.
  • Cleanup: original MIME, headers, and quoted history remain available on “Show original.”
